# 🧠 The Literary Circle Club — AI Agent Brain

Welcome to the AI Agent Brain repository for **The Literary Circle Club**.

This repository serves as the core intelligence, orchestration layer, and knowledge engine powering AI-driven experiences, discussions, recommendations, and club operations for The Literary Circle Club.

---

## 📖 Overview

The Literary Circle Club AI Agent is designed to act as an intelligent companion, moderator, and curator for our literary community. It assists members with literary discovery, facilitates rich book discussions, organizes club events, and provides contextual knowledge across classical and contemporary literature.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

- **📚 Literary Analysis & Prompt Generation**: Generates thought-provoking discussion questions, theme breakdowns, and character analyses for book club meetings.
- **🎯 Personalized Book Recommendations**: Matches members to their next favorite reads based on reading history, thematic preferences, and community trends.
- **🗓️ Club Event & Schedule Coordination**: Manages reading schedules, milestones, and meeting summaries.
- **💬 Conversational Agent**: Engages members with interactive discussions, trivia, and context on authors and historical background.
- **🧠 Knowledge Base Integration**: Centralized memory of past club reads, discussions, ratings, and member insights.
